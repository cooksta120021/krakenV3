Auto vs Custom (Simple Guide)

Auto (ML)

Auto means the bot updates the numbers for you (entry, take profit, stop loss, size, cooldown) based on recent market data. You mainly just choose the sleeve (Quiet vs Flash), the base asset, and click Train.

Custom (Manual)

Custom means you choose the numbers. The bot will not overwrite them. It will only follow your triggers.

What the Custom fields mean (dummy terms)

Entry drop %: How much cheaper you want the price to get before buying. Example: 0.50 means “buy if price dips 0.5% below the current price.”

Take profit %: How much profit you want before selling. Example: 1.00 means “sell when price is up 1% from where you would sell.”

Stop loss %: Your pain limit. Example: 0.50 means “if price moves against me by 0.5%, exit.”

Size %: How much of the sleeve’s allocated funds to use per trade. Example: 10 means “use 10% of the allocated amount.”

Cooldown s: A forced wait time after an order. Example: 300 means “wait at least 5 minutes after trading before doing anything else.”

What the Stage bar means

training: Refreshing/updating numbers.

waiting_buy: It is watching for a buy opportunity based on your triggers (Auto or Custom).

waiting_sell: It is watching for a sell opportunity based on your triggers (Auto or Custom).

placing_order: It is submitting an order now.

cooldown: Waiting out your cooldown before trying again.

paused: You paused it; it will not trade.

error: Something failed (usually order placement or a fetch). Check logs + Recent Orders.
